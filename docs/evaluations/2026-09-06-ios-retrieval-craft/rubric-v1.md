# Retrieval craft rubric v1 — frozen after initial implementation

Evaluate native Sessions and People for a busy relationship owner who must find
and reopen the right conversation/person without visual interruption. Score the
full, uncropped viewport, including shell chrome. Do not reward extra decoration,
artificial data, hidden state, or removal of necessary retrieval functions.

Five categories, each 0–20, total 0–100:

1. **Attention and clarity**: topic/person is the first read; context, recency,
   operational attention and read state are distinct; utility banners never
   dominate the content. A five-second scan answers what each row represents.
2. **Typography and legibility**: deliberate type hierarchy, comfortable reading,
   controlled line breaks, meaningful labels, no essential truncation, and
   no tiny text used to solve a layout problem.
3. **Alignment and rhythm**: consistent baselines/insets, coherent row grouping,
   balanced horizontal/vertical spacing, no stranded metadata or accidental
   blank line. Sparse data stays honestly sparse. Large type can scroll.
4. **Restraint and material**: scarce elevation, clear unread/read distinction,
   no redundant arrows or glyphs, elegant useful icons, warm neutral character,
   no glass orb or decorative border competing with the primary content.
5. **System coherence**: both pages share retrieval controls, predictable active
   scope/reset, readable light/dark variants, and credible accessibility layouts.
   Identity, menus, and status survive changes of state.

Category anchors: 0–7 broken/confused; 8–13 substantial visible defects; 14–16
usable with several rough edges; 17–18 polished with specific residual issues;
19 exceptionally coherent with only a minor refinement; 20 no identifiable
improvement that matters within the reviewed scenario. Distinguish observed
facts from preference, and deduct only against the criteria above.

Record each category's points, the total, exact image evidence, every deduction,
and at most three highest-impact corrections. The total is a subjective craft
judgment, not a measurement of human preference or a cross-reviewer average.
Do not infer tap behavior or accessibility conformance from still images.

Separate functional gates: search and filter both pages, reset empty results,
read/unread semantics, 44-point command targets, page navigation, content access,
identity/provenance integrity, and no unexpected external writes. A failed gate
cannot be outweighed by a visual score. Missing proof must be stated.

Use the same weights and anchors across rounds. Preserve all reviews, including
low scores. Reviewers work independently and do not receive previous scores.
