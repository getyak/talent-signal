# Relationship workflow comparison

This is a controlled synthetic task walkthrough, not a recruiter field study.
It makes no speed, adoption, or time-saved claim. Its purpose is to expose where
the RC3 product removes cross-surface reconciliation and where it adds necessary
review cost.

Task held constant: a recruiter sees a candidate message saying they need the
exact remote-work policy before Wednesday because another process moved earlier,
and must decide what this means and what to do next.

## Conventional desktop route

Observed/modelled sequence for the same task:

1. read the message in mail, Slack, or another conversation surface;
2. copy the relevant sentence to notes or working memory;
3. find the correct candidate record;
4. find the current Pursuit and infer whether this is the same relationship;
5. inspect earlier context for an existing dependency;
6. decide whether the sentence is a fact, preference, constraint, or merely
   availability;
7. add or update a CRM/ATS note or gap;
8. draft a response in the communication surface; and
9. manually reconcile whether the note, draft, and original evidence agree.

Expected surface switches: four to six. Manual joins: source → person, person →
Pursuit, evidence → claim, claim → follow-up, and local draft → sent state. A
later reviewer usually cannot reconstruct the exact sentence, identity choice,
or action boundary from one owned receipt.

## RC3 Relationship Workbench route

Direct frozen-Release sequence:

1. open Quick Panel; it contains zero implicit context;
2. select and separately confirm the exact Person/Pursuit/relationship scope;
3. add one explicit source to the visible Capsule;
4. choose and separately confirm who authored the excerpt;
5. review upload, retention, redaction, removal, and local-only boundaries;
6. submit one immutable manifest;
7. receive one evidence-backed dependency, governed proposal, clarification,
   or intentional `no_action`;
8. if a proposal exists, review the before/proposed diff and exact evidence,
   then make one explicit item decision;
9. read the canonical receipt in Action Center; and
10. optionally prepare a local draft whose receipt never says sent.

Expected surface switches: zero or one until an optional local draft is handed
off. Canonical joins are explicit and reviewable rather than inferred. The
cost is real: the recruiter performs scope and attribution review before first
value. RC3 earns its place only when relationship ambiguity or future audit
value is worth that cost.

## Directly evidenced product advantage

- Opening the surface does not capture another app or clipboard.
- One Capsule makes the considered source and excluded source visible.
- Relationship binding and source authorship are two different decisions.
- `no_action` is a settled result with a bounded Run and zero external effects,
  not an empty chatbot answer.
- Proposal approval, canonical mutation, receipt, and local draft handoff remain
  distinct.
- Action Center reopens the exact Task/operation projection rather than creating
  duplicate work.
- Ending the Task or stopping intake gives an explicit local deletion receipt.

## What remains unproven

- whether independent recruiters will accept the extra review steps at real
  work pace;
- whether the relationship insight is consistently better than their current
  reconstruction method;
- the frequency of this episode across boutique-search desks; and
- retention, week-four reuse, or willingness to grant system-window permission.

Those questions require a real-recruiter study. RC3 is evidence for interaction
coherence and technical trust, not field-market validation.
