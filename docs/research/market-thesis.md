# Market thesis: AI-era executive search

## Core thesis

Executive search is not CV transfer. Its durable value is reducing decision uncertainty, assessing motivation and fit, and carrying trust between a company and a scarce candidate.

## Implications

- AI lowers the cost of sourcing and administration, raising the value of verification, judgment, and trusted follow-through.
- US/EU workflows demand LinkedIn, email, ATS, and CRM interoperability.
- Chinese workflows have more private-chat context, making intentional mobile screenshot import a viable wedge, with high privacy expectations.
- Product value comes from answering who needs attention, what changed, what must be resolved, and what should happen next — not from producing more summaries.
