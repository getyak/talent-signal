Talent Signal — relationship workspace

macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

1. Drag Talent Signal to Applications, then open it.
2. Paste the HTTPS workspace address from your workspace owner.
3. Sign in with your own account.

The download includes no server, account, or private workspace address.
Private workspaces need the Mac on the same Tailscale network first.

Preview packages are ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized. Gatekeeper may
block them; do not disable Gatekeeper or strip quarantine. Use a signed,
notarized release for ordinary distribution.

Downloads and instructions:
