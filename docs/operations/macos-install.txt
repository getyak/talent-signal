Talent Signal — relationship workspace

Requires macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple silicon and Intel.

1. Drag Talent Signal.app to Applications, then open it.
2. Enter your workspace's HTTPS origin (scheme + hostname + optional port,
   without /workspace or other paths). Obtain it from your workspace owner.
3. Sign in with your own account. A private Tailscale workspace requires an
   authorized device on the same Tailnet and Tailscale connected first.

The download includes no server, account, credentials, or private workspace
address. Installing it does not grant access to anyone else's data.
Use the network button in the workspace toolbar to change the server.
Settings > Talent Signal links to release downloads and installation help.
Signed releases check at launch and periodically while running. A verified update
appears beside the account avatar. Click Update and Restart once to download,
verify and restart; there is no second application confirmation. Settings >
Updates shows the installed version and check status. Offline checks retry later.
Unconfigured previews need one manual trusted installation to enable updates:
quit the app and replace it in Applications. Preferences and workspace accounts
are retained. Keep the prior download for operator rollback.

Preview packages are explicitly labeled preview. They are ad-hoc signed and
not notarized by Apple; Gatekeeper may block them. Do not disable Gatekeeper or
remove quarantine to make a package look trusted. Use a signed release for
ordinary distribution, or build the preview yourself from its tagged source.
