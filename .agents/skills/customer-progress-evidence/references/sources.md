# Public source map

Accessed 2026-08-07.

## Primary and first-party sources

1. The Re-Wired Group, [About](https://therewiredgroup.com/about/)
   - Identifies Bob Moesta as a founder and principal architect of Jobs to Be
     Done and describes the firm's customer-progress focus.
2. The Re-Wired Group, [What is the Jobs To Be Done
   framework?](https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/complete-guide-jobs-to-be-done/)
   - Public overview of context, struggling moments, pushes and pulls,
     anxieties and habits, desired outcomes, criteria, and tradeoffs.
3. The Re-Wired Group, [The six stages of the buyers'
   timeline](https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/the-six-stages-of-the-buyers-timeline/)
   - First thought, passive looking, active looking, deciding, onboarding, and
     ongoing use.
4. The Re-Wired Group, [Demand-Side Sales
   101](https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/demand-side-sales-101/)
   - Reframes selling as helping customers buy and make progress.
5. The Re-Wired Group, [You can't persuade anyone to
   buy](https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/you-cant-persuade-anyone-to-buy/)
   - Treats the forces of progress as living context and extends progress work
     through implementation and new habits.
6. The Re-Wired Group, [The Top 10 JTBD Interview
   Tips](https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/the-top-10-jtbd-interview-tips/)
   - Emphasizes intent, context, listening, and customer language.
7. The Re-Wired Group, [Should you use AI to summarize your
   interviews?](https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/should-you-use-ai-to-summarize-interviews/)
   - Warns that AI classification and summarization can strip away intended
     meaning and context.

## Interpretation note

These sources describe a commercial consulting and training methodology. Use
their public concepts as a bounded review lens. Do not reproduce protected
materials, imply certification, or treat first-party effectiveness claims as
independent validation.
