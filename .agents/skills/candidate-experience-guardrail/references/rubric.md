# Candidate experience rubric

## 1. Agency and informed control

- **0:** Hidden or irreversible action affects the candidate.
- **1:** Choice exists but consequences are unclear.
- **2:** Confirmation exists with weak edit/undo.
- **3:** Consequences, edit, dismiss, and undo are clear.
- **4:** Control is proportionate, accessible, and available throughout the journey.

## 2. Human connection

- **0:** Deceptive human simulation or automated pressure.
- **1:** Generic automation dominates.
- **2:** Human contact exists but context is lost.
- **3:** Automation protects time for attentive human follow-through.
- **4:** Technology strengthens listening, continuity, and mutual understanding.

## 3. Communication quality

- **0:** Blaming, manipulative, misleading, or insensitive.
- **1:** Bland, generic, or falsely empathetic.
- **2:** Clear but impersonal or poorly timed.
- **3:** Specific, candid, considerate, and channel-aware.
- **4:** Communication also acknowledges uncertainty and gives the recipient an easy response path.

## 4. Time and attention respect

- **0:** Repeated or unnecessary interruption.
- **1:** Product optimizes recruiter convenience at others’ expense.
- **2:** Some prioritization, but delay ownership is unclear.
- **3:** The right person is prompted at the right time for a material reason.
- **4:** The product prevents avoidable waiting and protects all parties’ attention.

## 5. Collaboration accountability

- **0:** Candidate bears the cost of internal dysfunction.
- **1:** Ownership is hidden.
- **2:** Owner exists but escalation or SLA is weak.
- **3:** Recruiter and hiring-manager responsibilities are visible.
- **4:** The workflow anticipates handoff failure and offers a humane recovery path.

## 6. Dignified failure and rejection

- **0:** Ghosting, exposure, or harmful automated rejection.
- **1:** Abrupt dead end.
- **2:** Status is clear but support is weak.
- **3:** Honest closure, reason where appropriate, and next steps.
- **4:** Closure is timely, respectful, privacy-preserving, and operationally reliable.

## Verdict

Any deceptive automation, coercion, or hidden consequential action is a veto regardless of average score.
