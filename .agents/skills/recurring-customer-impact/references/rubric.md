# Recurring customer impact rubric

Score each applicable criterion from 0-4. Use `null` when customer-specific
context or outcome evidence is absent.

## Situation and pain

- **0:** Generic account data or seller assumptions replace customer context.
- **1:** A vague pain label has no episode or stakeholder.
- **2:** Context and friction exist but causality or scope is weak.
- **3:** Current situation and observable pain are customer-grounded.
- **4:** Changes, constraints, counterevidence, and affected work are versioned.

## Impact evidence

- **0:** Fabricated ROI, emotion, success, or consequence.
- **1:** Generic benefit or seller revenue is called customer impact.
- **2:** A relevant outcome exists without baseline, owner, or verification.
- **3:** The customer confirms a material, observable impact.
- **4:** Quantified claims include source, unit, baseline, population, horizon,
  and uncertainty.

## Critical event

- **0:** Seller pressure or quarter-end becomes false urgency.
- **1:** A date appears without consequence.
- **2:** A plausible milestone lacks owner, evidence, or current validity.
- **3:** A real event, consequence, owner, and timing are supported.
- **4:** Dependencies, expiry, change history, and resolution evidence are clear.

## Decision

- **0:** Authority, criteria, or process is invented.
- **1:** One contact or generic stage replaces the decision.
- **2:** Some people, criteria, steps, or tradeoffs are known.
- **3:** Relevant decision participants, criteria, process, and controls are
  supported.
- **4:** Conflicts, exceptions, changes, and observable completion are included.

## Lifecycle and handoff

- **0:** Signature erases promises, context, or customer ownership.
- **1:** A generated summary or CRM field becomes handoff truth.
- **2:** Promised impact reaches the next team with missing evidence or unknowns.
- **3:** Source-linked promises, criteria, dependencies, and permissions survive.
- **4:** Implementation and success evidence update or correct the original
  impact hypothesis.

## Recurring impact action

- **0:** Commercial pressure ignores customer outcome.
- **1:** Generic adoption, renewal, or expansion activity.
- **2:** A relevant action lacks outcome link, owner, timing, or verification.
- **3:** A minimal step validates, delivers, restores, or measures impact.
- **4:** Customer correction and observed outcome close the loop and improve the
  next lifecycle decision.

## Verdict

Return `abstain` on impact, urgency, success, renewal, or expansion claims when
the customer-specific causal link is missing. Fabricated impact, manufactured
critical events, revenue mislabeled customer success, silent handoff truth, or
a synthetic health or probability score is a veto.
