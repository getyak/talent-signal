## Primary and first-party sources

1. Winning by Design, [The Modern Revenue Operating Model:
   SPICED](https://winningbydesign.com/spiced-framework/)
   - Public definitions of situation, pain, impact, critical event, and
     decision and their use across the revenue lifecycle.
2. Winning by Design, [How to
   Diagnose](https://winningbydesign.com/resources/blueprints/how-to-diagnose/)
   - Public blueprint summary emphasizing diagnosis before prescription,
     customer impact, urgency, and decision drivers.
3. Jacco van der Kooij, [The Operating Model for Recurring
   Revenue](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Research-Paper_-The-Fundamental-Models-of-Recurring-Revenue.pdf)
   - Public recurring-revenue thesis, cross-functional operating model, and
     customer impact after the initial sale.
4. Winning by Design, [Has SaaS Lost GTM
   Fit?](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Workshop-WbD-Has-SaaS-Lost-GTM-Fit.pdf)
   - Connects customer experience, recurring impact, and sustainable growth.
5. Jacco van der Kooij, [How AI will replace sales people and why it will start
   in 2025](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Winning-by-Design-How-AI-will-replace-sales-people-and-why-it-will-start-in-2025.pdf)
   - First-party AI-GTM thesis about buyer knowledge, process, systems,
     automation, and human oversight.

## Interpretation note

Winning by Design is a commercial firm, SPICED is a trademarked method, and the
sources contain first-party performance and future claims. Use the public
concepts as a review lens. Do not copy proprietary blueprints, imply
certification, or treat numerical forecasts and effectiveness claims as
independent facts.
