# Public source map

Accessed 2026-08-07.

## Primary and first-party sources

1. Huthwaite International, [In conversation with Neil
   Rackham](https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/in-conversation-with-neil-rackham)
   - Identifies Rackham as Huthwaite's founder and SPIN's creator and provides
     current public interviews about the method's development.
2. Huthwaite International, [The SPIN
   Methodology](https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/spin-methodology)
   - Public definitions of situation, problem, implication, and need-payoff
     questions plus obtaining commitment.
3. Huthwaite International, [The science behind SPIN
   Selling](https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/hubfs/Whitepapers%20-%202025/Whitepaper%20PDFs/The%20Science%20behind%20SPIN%20Selling.pdf)
   - First-party research history, implied and explicit needs, and Advance
     versus Continuation.
4. Huthwaite International, [Exploring the power of SPIN Selling
   questions](https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/blog/spin-selling-questions)
   - Public explanation of the four question types as a framework for
     meaningful dialogue.
5. Huthwaite International, [The CRM System: under-regarded,
   under-used](https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/hubfs/Whitepapers%20-%202025/Whitepaper%20PDFs/The%20CRM%20System%20%E2%80%93%20under-regarded%2C%20under-used.pdf)
   - Applies the objective distinction between Advance and Continuation to CRM
     records and meeting outcomes.
6. Huthwaite International, [Cutting through the noise with SPIN
   Selling](https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/blog/cutting-noise-five-steps-develop-win-win-sales-deal)
   - Listening, problem focus, meaningful next steps, and exploring a proposal
     before counter-proposing.
7. Huthwaite International, [The origins of SPIN
   Selling](https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/blog/origins-of-spin-selling)
   - Rackham's public account of the research question and behavioral approach.

## Interpretation note

SPIN is a commercial, trademarked methodology. Huthwaite's research-scale and
effectiveness statements are first-party claims. Use the public behavioral
distinctions as a review lens; do not copy protected training, imply
certification, or market the output as a validated SPIN assessment.
