# Selection and evaluation science rubric

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Construct/criterion | Undefined global quality | Label only | Plausible but underspecified | Observable, scoped, job/product relevant | Predeclared construct and independently defensible criterion |
| Job/task analysis | None | Generic competency | Indirect rationale | Direct job/task evidence | Replicated across target contexts and maintained over change |
| Standardization/reliability | Free-form intuition | Inconsistent procedure | Rubric without reliability proof | Anchored procedure and agreement measured | Stable across raters/forms/time with drift monitoring |
| Validity | No evidence | Face-valid claim | Borrowed/correlational evidence | Relevant out-of-sample evidence with limits | Local predictive/incremental evidence and replication |
| Fairness/accessibility | Ignored | Proxies likely | Policy-only review | Sliced performance, accessibility, consequences | Ongoing monitoring, accommodation, appeal, and decision-impact evidence |
| Uncertainty/abstention | False certainty | Confidence decoration | Some missing-data behavior | Calibrated uncertainty and abstention | Coverage-risk tradeoffs tested and user behavior validated |
| Eval-set quality | Happy-path anecdotes | Small homogeneous set | Some negative cases | Representative stratification and adversarial cases | Frozen held-out sets, rare harms, contamination and drift controls |
| Grader integrity | Self-ratifying opaque judge | Single global model score | Rubric-bound judge only | Mixed graders, human gold set, evidence citations | Order/repeat/blinding/calibration and disagreement governance proven |
| Outcome inference | Unsupported causal claim | Proxy metric marketed as outcome | Association with caveats | Correctly scoped operational outcome | Predeclared prospective or credible causal design with replication |

## Verdict

- `pass`: every material dimension ≥3 and no candidate-assessment veto.
- `pass_with_changes`: bounded design gaps with no consequential deployment claim.
- `fail`: prohibited candidate scoring, undefined consequential construct, no evidence chain, or gate hidden by an average.
- `abstain`: target population, criterion, sample, implementation, or results are unavailable.

Use dimension scores; do not compute a psychometric-looking composite unless it has a justified decision rule.
