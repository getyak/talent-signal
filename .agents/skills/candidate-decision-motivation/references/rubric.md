# Candidate decision motivation rubric

## Role scorecard

- **0:** No role outcomes; candidate is judged by vibe or résumé.
- **1:** Generic responsibilities.
- **2:** Partial outcomes or competencies without priorities.
- **3:** Clear `what`, `how`, and `by_when`.
- **4:** Necessary and sufficient outcomes, tradeoffs, and observable behaviors are explicit.

## Candidate-owned evidence

- **0:** Motivation is fabricated or inferred from sensitive information.
- **1:** Recruiter assumptions dominate.
- **2:** Candidate statements exist but provenance or modality is unclear.
- **3:** Explicit statements are cited and separated from interpretation.
- **4:** Changes and contradictions are preserved across time.

## Decision-condition coverage

- **0:** Product uses a generic close script.
- **1:** Only compensation or availability is considered.
- **2:** Several Five Fs are considered but unknowns are hidden.
- **3:** Relevant drivers and unknowns are visible.
- **4:** The governing one or two tradeoffs are validated with the candidate.

## Action alignment

- **0:** Action pressures the candidate or advances process blindly.
- **1:** Generic follow-up.
- **2:** Addresses a relevant topic without owner or deadline.
- **3:** Resolves a material condition with the right owner and timing.
- **4:** Also defines how both sides will know the condition is resolved.

## Risk handling

- **0:** Risk becomes an unsupported rejection or acceptance prediction.
- **1:** Risk label has no evidence.
- **2:** Evidence exists but no mitigation or alternative explanation.
- **3:** Risk, evidence, uncertainty, and mitigation are separate.
- **4:** Counterevidence and candidate correction are first-class.

## Verdict

Use `abstain` when no candidate-stated decision evidence exists. Use a veto for sensitive inference or similarity-based culture scoring.
