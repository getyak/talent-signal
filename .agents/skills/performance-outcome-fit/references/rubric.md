# Performance outcome fit rubric

Score each applicable criterion from 0–4. Use `null` if the required context is absent.

## 1. Outcome definition

- **0:** Success is expressed only as credentials, stage movement, or hire completion.
- **1:** Vague responsibilities are present without deliverables or time horizon.
- **2:** Some expected results are named but are not measurable or prioritized.
- **3:** Specific role outcomes and time horizons guide the decision.
- **4:** Outcomes are specific, prioritized, contextual, and usable after hire.

## 2. Comparable evidence

- **0:** The product makes a performance claim without evidence.
- **1:** It relies on titles, years, prestige, or confidence.
- **2:** It captures an accomplishment but not role, context, actions, or result.
- **3:** It captures comparable accomplishment evidence with provenance.
- **4:** It checks patterns across examples and exposes contradictory or missing evidence.

## 3. Candidate motivation

- **0:** Motivation is invented or reduced to compensation.
- **1:** Generic interest is treated as commitment.
- **2:** A stated preference is visible but not connected to the role.
- **3:** Explicit motivations and tradeoffs are linked to job realities.
- **4:** The flow tests mutual value and allows the candidate to reject a poor tradeoff.

## 4. Career-move quality

- **0:** The action pressures a lateral or unclear move.
- **1:** Employer benefits dominate; candidate value is boilerplate.
- **2:** Potential upside is named but unverified.
- **3:** The next step resolves whether the move offers growth, impact, or learning.
- **4:** The product makes both upside and costs legible and supports an informed decision.

## 5. Action quality

- **0:** Automatic or harmful action unrelated to decision uncertainty.
- **1:** Generic scheduling or follow-up.
- **2:** Relevant action but vague timing, owner, or success condition.
- **3:** Minimal, timely, owned, reversible action resolving a material gap.
- **4:** The action also defines the observable outcome that will close the loop.

## 6. Longitudinal learning

- **0:** No result is recorded after confirmation.
- **1:** The product records completion only.
- **2:** It records whether the candidate advanced.
- **3:** It records whether the blocker or uncertainty was resolved.
- **4:** It compares predicted fit/value with later candidate and manager outcomes.

## Overall verdict

- `fail` if criterion 2 or 3 scores 0.
- `abstain` on candidate-quality questions without role outcomes and comparable evidence.
- Otherwise emphasize the weakest material criterion, not the arithmetic mean.
