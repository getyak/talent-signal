# Public source map

Accessed 2026-08-07.

## Primary and first-party sources

1. Challenger, [The Challenger Customer: Mobilizers, Talkers, and
   Blockers](https://challengerinc.com/the-challenger-customer-profiles/)
   - Public overview of joint authorship, buying-group research, customer
     profiles, and customer behavior versus seller activity.
2. Challenger, [Understanding The Challenger
   Customer](https://challengerinc.com/blog/entertaining-the-right-people-understanding-the-challenger-customer/)
   - Frames complex sales as consensus purchases and distinguishes accessible
     contacts from stakeholders who support organizational action.
3. Challenger, [Growing B2B Buying Groups Are Driving Purchasing
   Gridlock](https://challengerinc.com/blog/buying-group-gridlock/)
   - Buying-group complexity, conflicting concerns, group gridlock, and
     no-decision risk.
4. Challenger, [More B2B Decision Makers Are Weighing
   In](https://challengerinc.com/blog/more-b2b-decision-makers-want-in/)
   - Explains why integrated solutions require broader customer consensus and
     why more seller activity is not the same as a better decision process.
5. Challenger, [Resolving Buying Group
   Conflict](https://challengerinc.com/webinars/wtcs-season-3-resolving-buying-group-conflict/)
   - Public webinar summary focused on candid discussion of business problems
     and group consensus.
6. Challenger, [Company History](https://challengerinc.com/our-story/)
   - Public lineage from CEB through Gartner and Challenger and the method's
     commercial and trademark context.

## Interpretation note

The Challenger Customer is joint commercial work by Brent Adamson, Matt Dixon,
Pat Spenner, Nick Toman, and colleagues. Public company pages include
first-party statistics, archetypes, and effectiveness claims. Use them as a
bounded hypothesis and review vocabulary. Do not reproduce training, imply
certification, or store archetypes as person-level truth.
