# Buying group consensus rubric

Score each applicable criterion from 0-4. Use `null` when the initiative or
stakeholder evidence is insufficient.

## Stakeholder specificity

- **0:** The account is treated as one mind or people are invented.
- **1:** Names and titles appear without stakeholder-owned evidence.
- **2:** Several perspectives exist but scope, source, or time is weak.
- **3:** Each material stakeholder has separate, sourced goals and concerns.
- **4:** Perspectives remain versioned across initiatives, changes, and
  contradictions.

## Disagreement visibility

- **0:** Conflict is hidden or agreement is fabricated.
- **1:** Generic alignment language masks different priorities.
- **2:** Differences are named without criteria or consequence.
- **3:** Material agreements, conflicts, and unknowns are explicit.
- **4:** The map shows which evidence or tradeoff could resolve each conflict.

## Decision structure

- **0:** Title or seniority is assumed to equal authority.
- **1:** One contact substitutes for the process.
- **2:** Some criteria, steps, or approvals are present.
- **3:** Relevant people, criteria, sequence, resources, and controls are
  supported.
- **4:** Changes, exceptions, procurement constraints, and completion evidence
  are visible.

## Consensus-building behavior

- **0:** Personality, sentiment, or responsiveness becomes an influence label.
- **1:** Seller access is misreported as customer movement.
- **2:** A stakeholder appears supportive but observed group action is unclear.
- **3:** Customer-owned behavior demonstrably advances internal understanding.
- **4:** The group pressure-tests evidence, resolves a material conflict, and
  records an informed decision.

## Missing voices and permissions

- **0:** Confidential evidence leaks or affected people are deliberately hidden.
- **1:** The map claims completeness without a basis.
- **2:** Missing stakeholders are noted but consequence or access boundary is
  unclear.
- **3:** Missing voices, their relevance, and authorization limits are visible.
- **4:** The next step can safely obtain or consciously proceed without the
  missing perspective.

## Consensus action

- **0:** Manipulation, triangulation, or coercive pressure.
- **1:** Generic multi-threading serves only the seller forecast.
- **2:** A relevant group action lacks owner, timing, or verification.
- **3:** A minimal artifact or conversation resolves one consensus dependency.
- **4:** The customer can use, correct, decline, and verify it independently.

## Verdict

Return `abstain` on group completeness or influence when material evidence is
missing. Permanent archetypes, unsupported politics or authority inference,
confidential leakage, manipulative triangulation, or an influence score is a
veto.
