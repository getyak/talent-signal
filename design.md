# Design

The canonical cross-platform design judgment lives in
[`docs/design-system.md`](docs/design-system.md).

For marketing and web narrative work, also read
[`docs/reference/web-experience.md`](docs/reference/web-experience.md).

Talent Signal should feel like quiet relational intelligence:

- evidence before interpretation;
- change and dependency before completeness;
- one calm next step before broad analysis;
- explicit control at consequential moments;
- history and provenance through progressive disclosure;
- no candidate scoring or generic AI decoration.

Exact tokens, components, assets, animation behavior, responsive choices, and
performance constraints belong in the implementation and its tests. Match the
surrounding product rather than duplicating those details here.
