# Public source map

Accessed 2026-08-04.

1. ghSMART, [Who: The A Method for Hiring](https://ghsmart.com/insights/who-the-a-method-for-hiring/)
   - Public overview of outcome-driven scorecards and the proprietary Who Interview.
2. ghSMART, [Executive Selection](https://ghsmart.com/solution/executive-selection/)
   - Scorecard as objective yardstick; outcomes, competencies, behavior patterns, risks, and mitigation.
3. Jennifer Sturman and Samantha Hellauer, [Scorecards: They’re Not Just for Hiring Anymore](https://ghsmart.com/team_insights/scorecards-theyre-not-just-for-hiring-anymore/)
   - `what`, `how`, and `by when`; alignment, onboarding, performance, and living scorecards.
4. Sean McKean et al., [Hiring in the Time of Coronavirus: Selling Candidates Virtually](https://ghsmart.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Hiring-in-Time-of-Coronavirus-Part-2_Selling-candidates-virtually-May-2020.pdf)
   - Public Five Fs framework: Fit, Family, Freedom, Fortune, and Fun.
5. Jennifer Sturman and Rens van den Broek, [Hiring for Cultural Fit](https://ghsmart.com/team_insights/hiring-for-cultural-fit-a-simple-3-step-formula/)
   - Translate culture into observable behaviors and include them in a role scorecard.
6. ghSMART, [Dr. Geoff Smart](https://ghsmart.com/team/dr-geoff-smart/)
   - Public professional background.
