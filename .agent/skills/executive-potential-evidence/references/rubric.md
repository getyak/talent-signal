# Executive potential evidence rubric

## Evidence sufficiency

- **0:** Trait judgment from appearance, prose style, or one episode.
- **1:** Self-description without behavior or target role.
- **2:** One or two concrete episodes but weak context or counterevidence.
- **3:** Multiple episodes, outcomes, target complexity, and independent corroboration.
- **4:** Longitudinal patterns, counterexamples, development context, and local criterion linkage.

## Motivation

Assess commitment to the target responsibility and values, not generic ambition. Score 0 for invented motives and 4 for repeated, costly choices supported by evidence.

## Curiosity

Look for seeking new knowledge, candid feedback, experimentation, and willingness to change. Do not count verbosity, novelty, or question frequency alone.

## Insight

Look for connecting disparate information, making sense of ambiguity, simplifying complexity, and updating a view. Do not count eloquence alone.

## Engagement

Look for understanding others’ motives and creating shared commitment. Do not equate charm, extroversion, similarity, or social fluency with engagement.

## Determination

Look for persistence, recovery, ingenuity, disciplined focus, and responsiveness to disconfirming evidence. Do not equate overwork or stubbornness with determination.

## Development conversion

- **0:** Potential language ignores opportunity and experience.
- **2:** Development experiences are noted but not linked to competence.
- **4:** The product shows how specific experiences converted or failed to convert potential into capability.

## Verdict

Abstain unless evidence sufficiency is at least 3. Never average a score to bypass a sufficiency failure.
