# Inclusive sourcing recall rubric

## Work concept

- **0:** Search starts from an unreliable title or job-description dump.
- **1:** Requirements are copied as keywords.
- **2:** Core concepts are identified but not challenged.
- **3:** Actual work, outcomes, adjacent backgrounds, and candidate language are modeled.
- **4:** Subject-matter input and known relevant examples calibrate the concept.

## Recall design

- **0:** Required terms exclude plausible qualified people.
- **1:** Basic synonyms only.
- **2:** Several variants without coverage rationale.
- **3:** Explicit, indirect, and dark-matter signals are tested.
- **4:** Search variants map mutually informative segments and estimate misses.

## Ranking resistance

- **0:** Top rank is treated as quality.
- **1:** Only first pages are reviewed.
- **2:** Some deeper sampling.
- **3:** Top, middle, bottom, sparse, and atypical results are sampled.
- **4:** Ranking sensitivity is measured and used to improve the product.

## Exclusion audit

- **0:** Protected traits/proxies or unexplained exclusion.
- **1:** Exclusions optimize convenience.
- **2:** Exclusions are documented.
- **3:** Each exclusion has a job-relevant rationale and false-negative check.
- **4:** Exclusions are versioned, reversible, and monitored across relevant slices.

## Experiment quality

- **0:** Vendor or AI claim accepted without evidence.
- **1:** Anecdotal query success.
- **2:** Results counted without relevance sampling.
- **3:** Controlled variants, labeled samples, precision and recall proxies.
- **4:** Repeated tests with local outcome feedback and uncertainty.

## Candidate respect

Score 0 for invasive enrichment or manipulative engagement; score 4 for transparent, proportionate data use and specific, respectful outreach.
