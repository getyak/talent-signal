# GET-22 automatic relationship film

## Outcome and boundary

A new visitor does nothing and sees a recognizable capture become one person and
an unexpected connection within five seconds. The result, people and explanation
must fit together on short desktop and mobile viewports. This is a synthetic
product-vision film, not live account access, research or contact mutation.
The user explicitly rejected click-gated value and requested additional agents.

## Evidence and direction tree

D0: a person encountered in IM has scattered background; the visitor should feel
recognition when the product reveals a path through someone already in their world.
The previous design hid its mechanism behind a button and put the mobile outcome
below the fold. Test counts cannot override this failed design judgment.

D1-A: travel through platforms into one's human world. Continuous person anchor,
capture close-up, source convergence, relational camera pullback. Anti-reference:
floating cards that wait for a click or a decorative network wallpaper.
D1-B: an unexpected connection first, then reverse through its supporting contexts.
Large discovery statement and paired portraits, followed by a reconstructed path.
Anti-reference: a text-heavy generic AI answer or prompt box.

D2-A1: one viewport-height film, followed by optional source exploration, existing
capture/living-person sections and access. Automatic introduction yields to the
visitor's explicit controls. A2: scroll-scrubbed pinned film. Reject A2 because it
requires work before discovery and can consume the visitor's scrolling intent.
A is the current lead; B survives as a rendered challenger. Independent direction
and motion-strategy agents both recommend A with the connection emerging by ~5 s.
References: Apple product films for shot emphasis, Family for tactile continuity,
and the previous Cosmos/mymind comparison for a personal context world.

## Implementation intent

Responsive DOM/Motion film rather than a baked video: text remains sharp and
localized, source removal propagates into the visible path, and reduced-motion
visitors receive a complete static discovery. No autoplay loop. Separate user
playback intent from viewport/document suspension. Replay never restores sources.
No focus movement or scroll ownership from automatic playback. Pause and replay
remain visible; optional detailed exploration takes explicit focus and stops film.
