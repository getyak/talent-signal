# GET-22 website optimization

Issue: https://linear.app/getyak/issue/GET-22/官网优化和设计

## Outcome and boundary

A visitor identifies the relationship CRM, its initial independent-recruiter audience,
and the demo entry in one viewport. One synthetic case demonstrates quote review,
human fact confirmation, source removal and invalidation. Add browser-language
negotiation, English/Chinese switching, real product/method/trust/pricing routes.
Pricing explains access availability; no invented prices, billing, subscriptions,
customer results or autonomous relationship inference. Existing authenticated
workspaces and research article content are outside this slice.

## Evidence and direction

Baseline: 3b0a91bd, isolated worktree `talent-signal-get22`.
Current homepage repeats authority copy across eight sections, prioritizes source
processing over relationship continuity, and automatically changes two demos.
The existing warm palette, brand geometry and keyboard controls are worth retaining.
Screenshots and direction sources: ../docs/evaluations/2026-09-08-get22-website/.

D0: busy independent recruiter returning to a conversation wants context without
reconstruction, a visible unresolved question, and one deliberate next step.
D1-A: a living relationship brief connects each changed sentence to its source.
Spatial grammar: editorial margin + readable document. Signature: remove a quote
and see its dependent next step disappear. Anti-reference: generic dashboard tiles.
D1-B: a relationship path reveals who could help a goal. Spatial grammar: horizontal
linked people. Signature: inspect a path's provenance. Anti-reference: decorative
node graph implying that people know each other. Challenger retained as product
explanation, not an autonomous inference claim.
D2-A1: value/live brief → process strip → FAQ → access (leading architecture).
D2-A2: source-first guided capture → extracted facts → relationship → access.
A2 repeats the existing processing emphasis and delays the CRM benefit; reject.
D3: render A's split first viewport and B's full-width relationship path plus their
mid-page continuations, then compare before production implementation.
References reviewed 2026-09-08: https://attio.com (visible CRM objects),
https://me.sh (person-first continuity), https://www.granola.ai (recognizable output).
Borrow mechanisms only; retain Talent Signal's explicit source and human boundaries.
UI/UX database's generic gradient/pink suggestion conflicts with the approved brand;
retain warm neutrals/ink/vermilion and use its focus/contrast/responsive guidance.
