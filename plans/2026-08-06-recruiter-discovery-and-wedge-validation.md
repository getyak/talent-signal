# Recruiter Discovery and Wedge Validation Knowledge Compilation

## Status

Completed on 2026-08-06.

## Outcome

Convert a private concept-feedback conversation and the resulting recruiter
discovery discussion into repository-safe evidence, provisional synthesis, and
a compiled research protocol for validating Talent Signal's candidate/client
relationship-continuity wedge.

## Boundary

In scope:

- preserve the useful observations without copying the private transcript;
- distinguish non-recruiter concept feedback from target-user field evidence;
- reconcile the new hypotheses with the current candidate-momentum wedge;
- define a reusable recruiter interview and concierge-test protocol;
- compile the reviewed protocol into `docs/research/`.

Out of scope:

- treating one friend's opinion as product-market validation;
- changing the canonical product audience or promise;
- storing names, chat text, candidate data, or client-confidential details;
- implementing product behavior or starting external outreach.

## Current evidence

- A non-recruiter participant repeatedly reframed the product from person
  profiling and logs to relationship memory, a human-network database, and an
  action-time reply assistant.
- The participant identified human instability, differentiation, proof,
  privacy, segmentation, adoption threshold, app-opening behavior, and
  wrong-target automation as material questions.
- The participant has no recruiting workflow evidence and did not use a
  product, so the source can test category comprehension and surface risks but
  cannot validate recruiter demand, willingness to pay, or retention.
- Current canonical product truth already prioritizes candidate momentum,
  temporal relationship state, intentional capture, evidence review, and
  independent action approval.
- The existing provisional synthesis still contains several privacy and
  authority questions that were resolved later on 2026-08-06 and should be
  pruned.

## Approach

1. Capture a repository-safe source record with explicit evidence limits.
2. Update the existing provisional synthesis rather than create a competing
   note.
3. Publish one focused research page that owns the recruiter discovery and
   wedge-validation protocol.
4. Route the generated page from the human-maintained knowledge map.
5. Compile, validate, and inspect both source and generated diffs.

## Milestones

- [x] Add the repository-safe source record.
- [x] Reconcile and deepen the existing provisional synthesis.
- [x] Publish and route the recruiter discovery protocol.
- [x] Compile the Wiki and run the documentation checks.

## Completion evidence

The task is complete when:

- the private conversation is represented without transcript content or
  participant identity;
- observations, interpretation, and validation status remain distinct;
- one generated research document defines what to ask a recruiter and what
  counts as stronger evidence than polite concept feedback;
- no unvalidated claim changes canonical product truth;
- `pnpm wiki:test`, `pnpm wiki:check`, and `pnpm docs:check` pass.
