## Evidence and scoring
Evaluate sign-in/settings, conversations, People/Memory, Today/time, captures/source review, and recovery. For each: task completion 25, state/recovery 25, clarity 15, visual craft 15, accessibility 15, responsiveness 5. A point is earned only from an enumerated check; unknowns cannot pass. Privacy, unauthorized writes, wrong identity, loss, duplicate effects, false success or inaccessible consequential paths veto the journey regardless of points. Specialist 0-4 rubric scores remain separate and are never averaged into this scorecard. Scores are internal rubric results, not field-user certification.

the required checks and current evidence gaps; no numeric awards are issued.
